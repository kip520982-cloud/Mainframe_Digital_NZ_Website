Web app

Industry DJ School CRM

The custom CRM we built from scratch for Industry DJ School, then switched the school off HubSpot. Seven SaaS seats across sales, service and marketing were replaced by one platform, taking about $1,950 a month of recurring tooling spend to zero. The live customer base, its products, deals and pipeline history came across with zero records lost.

It ships as one product: a unified WhatsApp, Instagram and email inbox, booking calendar, click-to-call, campaigns, Instagram publishing with grid preview, Meta Ads and the SEO pipeline. It has since grown into the school's control plane. It publishes the public website, it's the one place course prices and intake dates are set, it's where the AI agents are directed from, and it monitors every webhook and dependency the business runs on. Twenty-nine surfaces, one database, one deploy.

Products & courses

One master editor for prices and intake dates.

Course prices and intake dates are set in one place. Publishing writes them to Stripe and to the website, and the editor shows the exact wording visitors will see, so dates are never typed by hand twice.

AI agents

The console the AI agents are run from.

A voice agent on the main business line qualifies callers, answers from a knowledge base, books them in and hands to a human when it should. A WhatsApp agent works inbound leads end to end, alongside an Instagram DM bot.

The console shows each agent on live status, one-click calls from the business line and a voice preview of any line. Every agent change is propose-then-approve.

iOS app

The CRM on iPhone.

Jenna, the Agent Manager, directs the AI workforce from the phone. Ask what the agents have been doing, get email highlights, or tell it how an agent should behave. Nothing changes until you approve it.

Contacts, money, agents and the calendar sit in the tab bar. It ships as a Custom App through Apple Business Manager, installed by org assignment.
