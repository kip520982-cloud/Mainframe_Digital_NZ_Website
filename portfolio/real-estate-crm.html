Custom CRM · Real estate

Real Estate CRM

An AI-assisted CRM we built for a Barfoot & Thompson agent in South Auckland. It runs her whole working day from one private workspace: daily follow-ups, inbox prompts, AI-drafted re-engagement emails, listing advertising across property sites, and sales and commission tracking.

Every contact carries its conversation notes and next step, and the dashboard shows who to call today, what's coming up and recent contacts. AI suggests re-engagement plans and drafts the emails, but nobody is contacted until she reviews each draft and sends it from her own Gmail; the CRM sends nothing itself. Deal stages and commission figures stay under her control. It's currently in pilot.

Your day

Who needs your attention today.

Follow-ups that are due or overdue sit in one list, each with the conversation note, the channel and the date. One tap marks it done or reschedules it.

Follow-up emails

AI drafts. She decides.

AI suggests a re-engagement plan for past contacts, a personal check-in, then a useful local update, then a referral check-in, only where appropriate. Only contacts who've given email permission appear, and nobody is enrolled or emailed until she reviews the draft and creates it in Gmail herself.

Inbox

Recent mail, turned into prompts.

The inbox view reads recent mail and flags what may need a reply or is worth keeping an eye on. Deal stages and forecasts stay manual: email signals are only prompts to review.
