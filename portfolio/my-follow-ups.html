Web app · Internal tool

My Follow-Ups

My Follow-Ups is an internal tool we built for a South Auckland real estate salesperson: one private place for conversation notes, next steps and the follow-up messages she reviews and sends herself.

It brings her day into one workspace: who to call today, what's coming up, recent contacts, inbox signals and a manual sales and commission tracker. AI suggests re-engagement plans and drafts emails, but no one is contacted until she reviews each draft and sends it from her own Gmail. The app sends nothing itself. It's currently in pilot.

Your day

Who needs your attention today.

Follow-ups that are due or overdue sit in one list, each with the conversation note, the channel and the date. One tap marks it done or reschedules it.

Demo workspace with fictional contacts. The salesperson's name and agency are blurred.

Follow-up emails

AI drafts. She decides.

AI suggests a re-engagement plan for past contacts, a personal check-in, then a useful local update, then a referral check-in, only where appropriate. Only contacts who've given email permission appear, and nobody is enrolled or emailed until she reviews the draft and creates it in Gmail herself.

Demo workspace with fictional contacts. The salesperson's name and agency are blurred.

Inbox

Recent mail, turned into prompts.

The inbox view reads recent mail and flags what may need a reply or is worth keeping an eye on. Deal stages and forecasts stay manual: email signals are only prompts to review.

Built for between appointments.
