Our work

Things we've built.

OUR PRODUCT · LIVE

Every call answered, day or night.

The Answer Line is an AI phone answering service for NZ businesses, built for trades and owner-operators who can't always get to the phone. It picks up every call in a natural Kiwi voice, captures what the caller needs, where they are and how urgent it is, then texts the job straight to the owner.

  • Base package: answer, capture and text the job
  • Custom package: books appointments into your calendar, loads your CRM or job software, and sends confirmations and reminders
  • Answers as the business and never guesses prices or makes promises
