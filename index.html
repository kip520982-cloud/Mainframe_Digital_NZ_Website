Connected workflows

Less copy and paste. More done.

Pick a moment from a normal week and see what changes when your software passes the work along by itself. Same tools, fewer steps for your team.

Without it

  • Enquiry lands in someone's inbox or voicemail
  • Details get copied into a spreadsheet or CRM by hand
  • Follow-up depends on who remembers
  • Some enquiries quietly go cold

Connected

  1. Enquiry captured from the call, form or email
  2. Contact and job created in your CRM automatically
  3. Reply and follow-ups sent on a schedule you set
  4. You get a short summary on your phone
  • Twilio
  • Google Workspace
  • Microsoft 365

Without it

  • Job notes live in texts and paper
  • Invoice typed into the accounts later
  • Payment reminders sent by hand, if at all

Connected

  1. Job marked done in the tool your team already uses
  2. Draft invoice created in Xero with the right details
  3. Reminders go out until it's paid
  4. Paid status flows back to the customer record
  • Xero

Without it

  • Template copied and edited by hand
  • Emailed out, then chased for a signature
  • Signed copy saved somewhere, under some name

Connected

  1. Document filled in from the customer record
  2. Sent for signature through DocuSign
  3. Signed copy named and filed in Dropbox
  4. Record updated, next step triggered
  • DocuSign
  • Dropbox

Without it

  • Everyone tries a different chatbot on their own
  • Business info pasted into personal accounts
  • No shared rules, so results vary

Connected

  1. Claude or ChatGPT set up properly for the team
  2. Connected to the documents and email it needs
  3. Simple guidelines everyone actually follows
  4. Hands-on training, so it gets used every day
  • Claude
  • ChatGPT
